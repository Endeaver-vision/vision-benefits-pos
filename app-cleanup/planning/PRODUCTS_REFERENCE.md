## PRODUCT ORGANIZATION BY USE CASE

### For Pricing Calculation
1. Lens Type (determines base price): Single Vision, Bifocal, Progressive tiers
2. Material (adds surcharge): Plastic, Polycarbonate, High Index variants
3. Treatments (adds charges): AR Coating, Transitions, Polarized, etc.
4. Add-ons: Blue Light, Tint, Scratch Coating, UV, Edge Polish

### For Insurance Mapping
- Match product name from extraction
- Look up in this table to find EyeMed/VSP terminology
- Use terminology to find copay in InsuranceAuthorization

### For Display/Ordering
- Show customer friendly names: "Varilux Comfort", "Crizal Sapphire"
- Hide internal tier codes: "Tier 1", "QT"
- Show material options separately from lens type

## Notes

- **VSP Codes**: Two-letter codes (AD, NJ, KD, QT, etc.) used in VSP documents
- **EyeMed Tiers**: Tier numbers (1-5) or "Standard" used in EyeMed documents
- **Age Dependencies**: Polycarbonate free for under 19, charged for adults
- **Formula Benefits**: Some tiers have complex formulas like "$25 copay; 20% off retail less $120 allowance"
- **Variations**: Same product may appear with different terminology across documents
