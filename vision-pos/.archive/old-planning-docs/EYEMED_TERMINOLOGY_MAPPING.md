## KEY OBSERVATIONS

### Recent Additions (February 2026 - Auths_EOPs Update)
7 new terminology variations discovered in extended document set (Auths_EOPs batch, 22 additional EyeMed documents):
- **"Exam Services"** (section header) - Found in 18 newer benefit documents
- **"Contacts - Therapeutic (Medically Necessary)"** (merged terminology) - Found in 3 documents
- **"Contacts only"** (service restriction) - Multiple documents with restricted contact-only plans
- **"Frame - Retail"** (explicit retail designation) - Found in 3 documents
- **"Lens Options"** (section header) - Found in 18 newer benefit documents
- **"Lenses"** (simplified section header) - Found across all documents
- **"Frame, Lens/Lens Options"** (combined benefit structure) - Newer flexible plan design

**Key Finding**: All copay amounts, allowances, tiers, and benefit categories from original 26 documents remain consistent. New variations are primarily document structure/formatting changes and terminology organization refinements.

### Naming Consistency
- **Anti-Reflective Coatings**: Documents use "Anti Reflective" (no hyphen) most frequently
- **Progressive Tiers**: Standard 4-tier system (Tier 1-4) when quality levels differentiated
- **Contact Lens Services**: Consistent use of "Fit and Follow-up" terminology

### Age-Based Variations
Several benefits have age-dependent copays:
- **Polycarbonate**: Free for under 19, charged for 19+
- **Photochromic (Non-Glass)**: Free for under 19, charged for 19+
- **Progressive Tier 4**: One document has separate age listings

### Plan Flexibility
Plans vary significantly on:
- Contact lens allowances: $100-$450
- Copay amounts for same benefit: Range is large (e.g., Progressive Standard: $0-$135)
- Service restrictions: Most plans restrict "either contacts OR frames"; some allow both

### Missing/Not Found
- **Trivex**: Not mentioned in any extracted document
- **Hi-Index 1.67/1.74 specific**: Bundled under "High Index" without specific index notation
